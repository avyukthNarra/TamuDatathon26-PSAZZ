## Definitions

- SE = sum over 5 classes of (pred - truth)^2.
- p_win = 1 - p_0. WIN = 2 * (p_win_pred - p_win_true)^2. SIZE = SE - WIN.
- Perfect-win (PW_SE): p_0 set to truth, the non-zero classes rescaled to keep the prediction's split and sum to 1 - truth p_0; PW_SE = SE over the non-zero classes only.
- Predictions with zero non-zero mass (truth split fallback used): 0.
